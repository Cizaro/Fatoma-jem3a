# Quizzes

Three sizes. Use the right one at the right moment.

## 🟢 Mini-quizzes — after every lesson
`mini-quizzes/lesson-01.md` … `lesson-10.md`

8 questions, 5 minutes, done at the end of the Zoom call while it's still fresh.
Each one ends with a line telling you whether to move on or redo something.
**These are the ones you'll use most.**

## 🟡 Unit quizzes — after every 2–3 lessons
| File | Covers | When |
|---|---|---|
| `quiz-00-intro.md` | lesson 0, no code at all | before or right after session 1 |
| `quiz-01-basics.md` | lessons 1–2 | after session 2 |
| `quiz-02-logic.md` | lessons 3–4 | after session 4 |
| `quiz-03-loops-arrays.md` | lessons 5–7 | after session 7 |
| `quiz-04-forms-mysql.md` | lessons 8–10 | after session 10 |

15 questions each, 20–25 minutes. These check that things stuck a week later, not just
in the moment.

## 🔴 Final exam
`final-exam.md` — 40 marks, 90 minutes, closed book, real exam conditions.

Do this once you've finished lesson 10, and again a week before the real exam.
The score matters less than **which section** loses the marks — the answer key explains
what each weak section means and what to do about it.

## How to actually use a quiz

1. **Close everything.** Lesson files, the cheat sheet, the browser. A quiz taken with the
   answers open teaches you nothing except that you can read.
2. **Write the answer even when you're unsure.** A wrong answer you committed to is worth
   ten "I think maybe"s — it shows you exactly where your idea of PHP differs from reality.
3. **Mark it straight away**, while you still remember why you answered that way.
4. **Only discuss the wrong ones.** Going over what you already got right is wasted time.
5. **Keep the list of question numbers you got wrong.** That list is your revision plan —
   much better than re-reading everything from the start.
