  • Sections 1 to 3: no computer. No notes, no lesson files, no running the snippets. Trace them in your head — that is the thing being examined.
  • Section 4 you write in VS Code, exactly as you would at home. The page gives you the starter file and tells you where to put it. Paste it back when the code runs.
  • Have paper and a pen next to you before you start. The flowchart is drawn, not typed.
  • The clock starts when you press begin and does not pause. At 0:00 it marks what you have.
  • Sections 1, 2 and most of 3 are marked for you. The written answers you mark yourself, one criterion at a time, against the real scheme.
  • Be honest with the marking. A mark you gave yourself for an answer you didn't write buys you nothing — what you want out of this is the revision list, not the number.
  • Your answers save as you type, so a closed tab is not a lost attempt.
